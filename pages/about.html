How this book was made

The people, the purpose and the sources

✦About this edition

Inspired by James Joyce’s Dubliners, Retellings is a space for the bookworms, history buffs and travellers who want to look beyond the surface of the city. It began as a team project for the MSc in Interactive Digital Media at Trinity College Dublin, in the spring of 2026.

This edition, with its book of records, place windows and walking-tour planner, was redesigned by Nashwa Hamido.
